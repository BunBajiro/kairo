## Exact intent and bounded history

192 uses H4's strict version1 envelope: `version,token,runtime_epoch,storage_lineage,owner_connection,map_incarnation,revision,op`, with `op:"undo"` and exactly `target:{entry_id,entry_incarnation}`. Route map/pilot authority follows H4; no body aliases. Opaque identities use bounded server-issued forms. Require current positive connected owner and interactive owned map after complete body receipt and at commit. Reject duplicate/unknown keys, wrong types/null/arrays, generated/receipt echoes, client old state/snapshot/replacement, offsets/counts/family selectors and coercion before any cleanup or mutation. No optional provider collection or roster consent is required for local inverse work.

One map CAS and latest safe receipt own undo; entry/field generations are qualification metadata, **not per-entry command lanes**. The exact retained current top must match target identity/incarnation and immutable map/origin. Empty/non-top/stale/consumed/evicted/expired/ineligible or corrupt-unavailable targets reject unchanged. There is no successful absent/no-op undo, silent skip, implicit older target, redo or arbitrary historical checkout. A committed192 makes the declared inverse change, consumes exactly its target and advances current map authority/activity/latest receipt. Token reuse follows shared conflict/readback, never a second inverse. Old captured bases conflict after intervening commits even if an entry remains eligible for a new deliberate command.

Allocate immutable nonreused entry identity at its originating effective commit. Retain only bounded family, originating action/map base-result association, allowed field masks, exact affected row/resource identities and post-state qualifications, narrow original values/context, required dependency references/deadline, and exact retained predecessor associations. Private values stay in the private inverse owner; origin and payload cannot be rewritten by a client or relabelled after recreation. Qualification changes only through the accepted writer transitions below. Never retain whole maps, child notebooks/receipts, all ancestor payload copies or permanent consumed tombstones.

Keep source ceiling10 entries, subject to P0-04 measured per-entry/per-map/aggregate payload and reference budgets. Adding a required entry deterministically evicts oldest entries until count and byte retention fit, pruning exact links to lost ancestors. A single new entry that cannot fit rejects its originating undoable action atomically; never silently accept without undo, split a batch or evict live rows/archives. An entry can remain useful after an older ancestor is evicted, but that ancestor cannot be reconstructed. Accepted evidence-only originating no-ops preserve history, qualifications and deadlines and create no phantom inverse; they still advance authority/receipt as their owning contract requires.

An incompatible writer retires the precise affected entries and dependent qualification links in its own outer transaction; H4/H6/H7/H10 explicitly broader history-clears remain controlling. Ordinary compatible field successors retain their predecessors as below. Due expiry has a separate bounded owner transition, including restoration entries whose live row is absent. History retirement publishes a truthful current top and **advances map authority atomically**, without inventing a user receipt/token. A newly observed invalid/due top makes192 reject unchanged; rejected192 and passive inspection cannot retire it, pop it or apply an older entry. The bounded maintenance/writer owner must resolve this blocker and expose a separately committed top; only a new deliberate click may target it. No indefinitely stranded invalid top and no per-entry polling.
