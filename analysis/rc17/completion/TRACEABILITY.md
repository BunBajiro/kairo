## Limits and final acceptance

Historical RSS/heap-capacity growth in artificial same-process reconstruction remains an unresolved observation, not a proven leak or a release blocker by itself. Real updater replacement uses a fresh child. Short browser/Core retention runs cannot prove indefinite leak freedom. Software-rendered browser traces cannot establish this user's desktop GPU/OBS or fan behavior. Conditional future in-process rebuild requirements remain conditional.

Old failed fixture/browser-cleanup/build-identity attempts stay failed. The exact05d browser replay closes only that original mechanical gate. Surviving fa6 backend evidence does not certify reconstructed source, and a protocol SSE client does not prove renderer behavior. Small adverse observations remain visible even when below the prioritization threshold.

Before clean RC17: dispose **every** implementation/conditional row above; freeze one source/build/harness identity; complete affected correctness/type/build and compiled-browser gates; run matched local and independent CI performance lanes with full outputs/counters and all adverse rows; independently review evidence; then create and verify an extracted clean package.
